Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

Airport Guide Map does not collect, store on our servers, transmit to us, or share any personal data. Your privacy is built into the app's design:

  • There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking SDKs, and no advertising frameworks in the app.
  • Your saved guides, recent airports, and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
  • The airport database is built into the app, so step-by-step guides work offline. When you are online, the app contacts only public flight and weather data services — Open-Meteo (airport weather), the FAA airport status service (ground delays), and adsb.lol / adsbdb (live flight positions and routes) — and only for the airport or flight you are looking at. These requests carry no name, no account, and no personal identifier.
  • If you choose to add your own free FlightAware AeroAPI key in the app, it is stored only on your device and used only for your own flight lookups.
  • We never receive your data, so we cannot see, access, sell, or delete it.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at ava@avalimo.net.
