Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026

Weather3D does not collect, store on our servers, transmit to us, or share any personal data. Your privacy is built into the app's design:

  • Your saved city and unit preference (°F / °C) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
  • The app contains no analytics, no tracking SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no user accounts.
  • To show weather, the app sends the city name you search for to the public Open-Meteo weather services (forecast, historical archive, and air quality). These requests carry no name, no account, and no personal identifier — just the city you asked about.
  • We never receive your data, so we cannot see, access, sell, or delete it.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at ava@avalimo.net.
