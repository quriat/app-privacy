Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

AvaLimo is operated by AVALIMO LLC of Missouri City, Texas. We run the website avalimo.net and an internal social-media content tool that creates and publishes AvaLimo's own promotional content. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

The content tool connects only the operator's own social-media accounts — for example, AvaLimo's TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit — using the platforms' official APIs and only with the account owner's authorization. Through those connections, the tool accesses only:

  • Basic profile information for the connected account, such as the account name and avatar.
  • Account statistics, such as follower counts and video performance.
  • The connected account's video list, so existing posts can be reviewed and managed.
  • The ability to upload and publish AvaLimo's own promotional videos to the connected account, only at the operator's direction.

We do not sell, rent, or share any data obtained through these social-media integrations with third parties. No customer or passenger data is accessed through these integrations — the tool works only with the business's own social accounts and its own content.

Access tokens for connected accounts are stored securely by the integration provider and are used solely to carry out the actions described above. The operator can disconnect any social account at any time; disconnecting immediately revokes the tool's access to that account.
